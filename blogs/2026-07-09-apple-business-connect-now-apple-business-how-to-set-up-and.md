Apple Business Connect is now Apple Business. In April 2026, Apple combined Apple Business Connect, Apple Business Manager, and Apple Business Essentials into a single platform called Apple Business. Existing business listings, place cards, photos, and account settings migrated automatically, so organizations didn’t need to recreate their listings.
