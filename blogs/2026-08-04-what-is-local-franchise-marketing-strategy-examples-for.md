Local franchise marketing is the strategy of promoting a franchise brand while helping each location attract customers in its local market. It combines national marketing, led by the franchisor, with local marketing, executed by individual franchisees. Summary – As franchise businesses and multi-location local businesses expand, balancing brand consistency with local flexibility becomes more challenging.
