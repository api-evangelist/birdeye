A guide to managing patient reviews across UK NHS and private providers within regulatory frameworks including CQC governance, professional codes from GMC/NMC/GDC/GPhC/HCPC, and UK GDPR. It covers primary review platforms (NHS.uk, Care Opinion, iWantGreatCare, Doctify, Google Business Profile, Trustpilot, HealthUnlocked), a compliant request-and-response workflow with UK GDPR Article 9 consent capture, three response templates, and how AI can support healthcare review management with healthcare guardrails and sentiment analysis.
